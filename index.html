The heart of the ministry
God meets us in our brokenness, gives us a secure place to belong, and redeems even the most painful parts of our story.
From darkness into light

A story of longing, belonging, and the love that came for her.

The youngest of six children, Sara grew up in a home affected by alcoholism and family brokenness. At nine, she entered a children's home and later lived in a foster home. She learned early to strive, perform, and please others in an effort to secure the love her heart needed.

Through Jesus, Sara came to know God not only as Savior, but as Father. She discovered that the love she had spent her life striving for could never be earned. In the Father, it was already hers to receive. As striving gave way to rest, His love became the secure home she had been searching for and the source from which forgiveness, reconciliation, and a different future became possible.

“Though my father and mother forsake me, the Lord will receive me.” Psalm 27:10
About Sara

Honest stories. Gospel hope. A life being redeemed.

Sara M. Jenkins knows what it is to spend years striving to be chosen, accepted, and secure. Through her own journey from childhood brokenness to belonging, she came to know God not only as Savior, but as the perfect Father and learned to rest in His love, a love she did not have to earn but could simply receive.

A University of South Carolina graduate and former collegiate athlete in basketball and cross country/track, Sara began her career as a high school teacher and coach before following God’s call to speak and preach the gospel. She serves on three boards and advocates for several ministries, with a heart for people who feel overlooked. Whether in a group home or a grocery store, she wants people to feel seen and know the love God has for them. Time with the Father through prayer, silence, and solitude helps her serve from a place of rest.

A joyful learner at heart, Sara loves reading and is finishing her first book. She enjoys running, hiking with her husband, and long walks with her girlfriends. Prayer gardens and the beach are favorite retreats, and her family enjoys traveling and sporting events together. Sara delights in the life God has redeemed through Jesus, with hope that her children will carry forward a new family legacy rooted in His love, lighting the way for generations to come.

Signature messages

Truth that reaches the heart.

Sara adapts each message to the setting while keeping the gospel and the transforming love of God at the center.

01

Root Systems

Exploring the family patterns, childhood wounds, and old root systems that shape how we see ourselves and live. Through Christ, we are replanted into the family of God, given new roots in His love and grace, and invited to live as His sons and daughters.

Scripture: Colossians 2:6–7

02

Freedom From Shame

Through a powerful branding iron illustration, Sara explores how shame and rejection can keep us hiding and pretending, inviting audiences to bring these hurts into the light, receive God’s grace, and embrace who they are in Christ.

Scripture: Psalm 34:5

03

Forgiveness and Reconciliation

Forgiveness can be hard. Through a story of grace and mercy, Sara gently invites audiences to look into their hearts and address bitterness and unforgiveness that hinder fellowship with our Heavenly Father. With meekness and gentleness, we can entrust our wounds to Him, receive His healing grace, and make room for reconciliation where possible. Forgiven people forgive.

Scripture: Matthew 18:21–35

04

Intimacy With God

Moving beyond knowing about God to knowing Him personally and learning to remain in His presence and love through trusting obedience. Sara also teaches how spiritual disciplines help us make room for God, enjoy His presence, and commune with the Father.

Scripture: John 15:9–10

05

Approval, Value and Worth

Finding lasting acceptance in the Father rather than striving for it through comparison, performance, achievement, or religious effort. An invitation to slow down, make room for God, and receive the rest Jesus offers.

Scripture: Matthew 11:28

06

Loving With God’s Love

Serving vulnerable young people with truth, compassion, consistency, and the transforming love of the gospel.

Scripture: 1 John 4:19

Discipleship and gospel rest

Walking with others along the way of Jesus.

Discipleship is a relational journey of seeking God, learning His Word, asking honest questions, and applying gospel truth to everyday life. It is walking alongside someone as faith becomes personal, practiced, and deeply rooted.

Sara comes alongside students and women from all backgrounds who want to grow in their relationship with God, helping them bring hidden hurts into the light, receive God personally as Father, renew their minds, and take faithful steps forward. She shares Scripture, encouragement, and wisdom from her own journey while always pointing people back to Jesus.

Many people come to Christ yet still struggle to rest in the Father's love. As God becomes our source of love and security, we are freed from striving and able to love others with greater mercy and grace, including within marriage, family, and the relationships closest to us.

My Prayer for You

Root them deeply in Your love. Give them courage to walk in Your truth, grace to forgive, and hope for what You are redeeming in their lives. May they know You as the perfect Father and learn to live as Your beloved child.

In Jesus’ name, amen.
